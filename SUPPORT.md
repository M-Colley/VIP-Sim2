# Support

## Free community support

Ask questions and report bugs at https://github.com/M-Colley/VIP-Sim2/issues. Search existing issues first.

In the application, press F1, then "Report a problem". Use "Copy diagnostics path" to find the log files to attach. The logs stay on your machine until you attach them.

Report vulnerabilities privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Installation steps and permissions are in [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md). Accessibility barriers in VIP-Sim itself are bugs, and are reported the same way ([docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md](docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md)).

## Paid support

Organisations can contract support, team training, deployment help and custom condition profiles. See [docs/ORGANISATIONS.md](docs/ORGANISATIONS.md).
