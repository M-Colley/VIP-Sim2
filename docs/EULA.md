**It is not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, screen for, treat or monitor any
condition, and must not be used for clinical decisions or as evidence of accessibility

Each effect approximates a single symptom. Severity values are uncalibrated starting
points, not clinically validated measurements, and real conditions vary enormously between
individuals. Any claim that the Software shows "what condition X looks like" is the
claimant's, not the Licensor's.
